# Darkwatch: Darker Edition 0.7.9

Prepared for private release review. Publication is pending.

## Gameplay and animation

- Corrected the sniper rifle elbow and crossbow reload arm poses.
- Improved the dual-pistol neck and head position.
- Dropped magazines and cylinders respond to animated foot contacts without
  pushing the player around.
- Revised the male characters' free-arm walking motion, with a wider shoulder
  angle and separate crouch movement.
- Fixed airborne crouch input for ground pound. The ability requires its story
  rune; the testing override is disabled by default.

## Launcher and performance

- Added Skip Intro to reach profile selection directly.
- Press T during gameplay to cycle first-person, third-person and centered views.
- Reused prepared shader caches and reduced repeated content checks on launch.
- Release diagnostics remain off by default.

## Installation

Matching Windows and experimental Linux/Proton downloads use the same
runtime and conversion recipes. Your supported original Xbox Darkwatch disc
image is required. Game files, saves and the development test room are not
included in the downloads.

Existing saves, settings and optional texture packs are preserved during updates.
The separate 0.7 HD texture pack remains compatible.

## Verification

Windows testing covers the reported fixes in selected gameplay scenes. Full
campaign progression and every character/weapon combination have not been
exhaustively verified. Linux/Proton gameplay has not been verified for this
version and remains experimental.
