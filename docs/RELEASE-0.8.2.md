# Darkwatch: Darker Edition 0.8.2

Campaign fixes and gameplay improvements for Windows and experimental Linux/Proton.

## Gameplay and physics

- Fixed Redeemer switching after the opening chapter and repeated ammo notifications when switching weapons during pickups.
- Added melee interaction and grabbing for detached enemy parts, alongside walking, gunfire and explosion interactions.
- Improved corpse dragging and the first-person ragdoll death camera.
- Added a First-Person Body toggle in Graphics for looking down at your character.
- Enabled supported characters' jiggle physics during in-game cutscenes.
- Stopped destroyed Mines trees from continuing to scream.
- Repaired invisible ramps and their side surfaces in the Sewers.

## Presentation and controls

- Fixed rectangular character shadows and shadows showing through walls.
- Removed the misplaced sniper smoke puff while scoped.
- Preserved HUD, body and weapon visibility while paused.
- Blocked the weapon wheel during in-game cutscenes.
- Improved blood-stain detail while retaining the original subtle opacity.
- Corrected Capcom logo proportions and size.

## Installation

Download the Windows ZIP or experimental Linux/Proton archive below. Supply your own supported original Xbox disc; game files are not included.

Expanded characters, models and animations remain optional on fresh installations. Original-only installations retain the original playable characters and animations. Existing installations retain their content choice. Updated resources are prepared by the launcher; allow preparation to finish before playing.

The existing separate HD texture pack remains compatible. No updated texture pack is required for this release's floor-blood improvement.

## Verification and scope

Fresh Windows installation with expanded characters prepared successfully and reached campaign gameplay, confirmed through preparation checks and user testing. Repeated loading of the tested chapter did not reproduce progressive slowdown; no new campaign loading-speed improvement is claimed.

Windows and Linux packages contain the same game runtime. Linux/Proton remains experimental and has not received fresh gameplay verification for this update. Local co-op remains beta. Experimental online co-op is not included.

The source archive and the v0.8.2 tag match the audited source commit published on main. No original game assets or generated game source are included in the source archive.
