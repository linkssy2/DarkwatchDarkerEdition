## Darkwatch: Darker Edition 0.1

First public Windows x64 release, powered by **[XboxXBERecomp by TGP482](https://github.com/TGP482/Xbox-XBERecomp)**. Thanks also to **[sp00nz's xboxrecomp](https://github.com/sp00nznet/xboxrecomp)** for the earlier foundation.

- Native mouse aiming, mouse-friendly menus and editable bindings.
- Xbox, PS4 and Switch controller support with matching prompts.
- Widescreen, resolution/internal scale, FOV, MSAA, anisotropic filtering and more graphics options.
- Optional HD menus/fonts/HUD and enhanced intro slides. Videos remain original.
- Shader preparation, portable saves/settings, direct-start EXE and Quit to Desktop.

**Download:** Choose **Darkwatch-Darker-Edition-0.1-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the whole folder, and run **Darkwatch Darker Edition.exe**. Import your supported original Xbox ISO/XISO and wait for preparation. After setup, **Play Darkwatch.exe** launches directly. Normal play has no timer. No original disc assets, Xbox executable, ISO, movies, audio or saves are included. Approved HD enhancement artwork and launcher branding are included; the final texture pack is built from your verified import.

**Update from a candidate:** Extract to a new folder, copy your existing `GameData` folder, then use Import / Repair if verification asks. Keep the old copy until you have checked your saves.

**Notes:** Full-campaign, fresh-Windows and physical PS4/Switch testing remain outstanding. Switch uses B to select and A to go back; Xbox-emulating wrappers show Xbox prompts. Shader preparation improved the tested opening route, but unseen shaders and loading may still cause pauses. Routine logs, captures and development diagnostics are disabled; failures may leave a capped error report. See the [README](https://github.com/linkssy2/DarkwatchDarkerEdition#readme) for requirements and controls.

The **Source.zip** contains maintained source and SDK patches, excluding generated title code and original disc content. Build limitations and prerequisites are documented. **SHA256SUMS.txt** covers both downloads.
